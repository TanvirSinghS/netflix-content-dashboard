KEY INSIGHTS — Netflix catalogue, 2008 – 25 Sep 2021 (8,807 titles)

• Movies dominate the catalogue: 6,131 titles (69.6%) against 2,676 TV shows (30.4%) — roughly a 7:3 split in favour of film.

• Additions exploded after 2016: 429 titles that year, 1,188 in 2017 (+177%), then a peak of 2,016 in 2019 and 1,879 in 2020 (−7%). 2021 shows 1,498 but the data stops on 25 September, so it is not a full year.

• July is the busiest release month overall with 827 titles, just ahead of December (813) and September (770); 2021's own peak was also July (257). September–December totals are understated because 2021 is partial.

• The United States is the largest supplier with 3,690 country tags, ahead of India (1,046) and the United Kingdom (806); 831 titles have no country recorded and are excluded from that chart.

• International Movies is the most common genre tag (2,752 titles, ~31% of the catalogue), followed by Dramas (2,427) and Comedies (1,674). 6,787 titles carry more than one genre, so genre counts add up to more than 8,807.

• The catalogue skews adult: Adults 4,009 (45.5%), Teens 2,650 (30.1%), Older Kids 1,150 (13.1%), Kids 908 (10.3%), Unrated 90 (1.0%). Movies average 99.6 minutes (median 98), and 74% run between 60 and 120 minutes.
